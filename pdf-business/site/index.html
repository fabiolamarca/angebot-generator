FAQ

How do I get the PDF?

Right after checkout you get a download link on screen and by email. Open it on your phone or print it.

How much protein is it per day?

Around 140 to 150 g per day following the plan as written. The PDF shows you how to scale it up or down to your own target in 30 seconds.

Is this a weight loss diet?

No. It is a protein-first meal prep system. The plan gives you a base of about 1,350 to 1,900 kcal per day, and you add sides to match your own goal, whether that is losing fat or building muscle.

I'm vegetarian. Will it work?

Week 3 is plant-forward and the swap guide shows how to replace meat in the other recipes. Dairy and eggs are used, so it is not fully vegan.

Do I need special equipment?

No. Two sheet pans, a big pot, a pan, a muffin tin and some containers. An air fryer works for the sheet-pan recipes too.

Is this medical advice?

No. It is general nutrition education for healthy adults. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant or take medication, check with your doctor or a registered dietitian first.
