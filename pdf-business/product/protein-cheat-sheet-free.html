3 quick wins

Hit your protein goal without trying harder

1. Fix breakfast first

Most people eat 10 to 15 g at breakfast. A Greek yogurt bowl or two egg muffins with a shake gets you to 30 g in under 3 minutes.

2. Cook protein in bulk, twice a week

One tray of chicken and one pot of chili on Sunday covers most lunches and dinners until Wednesday.

3. Keep a backup shelf

Canned tuna, Greek yogurt, eggs, cottage cheese and protein powder. When the plan breaks, you still hit your number.
